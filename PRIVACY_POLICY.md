# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sicae ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Sicae Discord bot (the "Bot") and its related support server (together, the "Service").

By adding the Bot to a server or interacting with it, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, remove the Bot from your server and stop using it.

## 1. Information We Collect

The Bot only receives data that Discord makes available to it through its API, and only what it needs to work.

- **User information:** your Discord user ID, username, display name, avatar, and the roles you hold in a server where the Bot is present.
- **Server information:** the server (guild) ID, name, channel IDs and names, role IDs, and the Bot's configuration for that server (such as prefixes, enabled features, and settings chosen by server admins).
- **Command and interaction data:** the commands, buttons, menus, and options you use with the Bot, and the content you submit as part of them.
- **Message content:** the Bot may read message content only to detect and respond to commands or to run features that server admins enable. We do not read or store message content for any other purpose.
- **Technical and log data:** timestamps, error logs, and command usage records used for debugging, abuse prevention, and keeping the Bot running.

We do not collect passwords, payment details, or private (direct) messages between other users. We do not ask for your real name, email address, or location.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Operate the Bot and carry out the commands and features you or your server admins request
- Store each server's settings so the Bot behaves consistently
- Prevent abuse, spam, and misuse, and enforce our Terms of Service
- Diagnose errors, monitor performance, and improve the Bot
- Respond to support requests
- Comply with legal obligations and Discord's requirements

## 3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

We may share information only:

- **With Discord,** because the Bot operates through Discord's platform and is subject to Discord's own policies
- **With service providers** that host the Bot or store its data, under confidentiality obligations
- **When required by law,** or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the Service
- **With your server's administrators,** where a feature naturally shows activity (such as logs or leaderboards that server admins have enabled)

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

- Server settings and related data are kept while the Bot is in your server.
- When the Bot is removed from a server, we delete that server's stored data within 30 days, except where we must keep it for legal or security reasons.
- Log data is kept only as long as needed for debugging and security, and is deleted periodically.
- You can ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you at any time (see Section 6).

## 5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data we hold, such as access controls and keeping secrets out of public code. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent.

To make a request, contact us on Discord at https://discord.gg/sicae. We may need to verify that the account making the request is yours, for example by asking you to message us from that Discord account.

You can also:

- Ask a server admin to remove the Bot from your server
- Stop using the Bot's commands and features
- Manage what data Discord shares with apps through your Discord privacy settings

## 7. Discord and Third-Party Services

The Bot runs on Discord. Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy apply to your use of Discord, and we are not responsible for how Discord handles your data. Any links the Bot posts may lead to third-party sites with their own policies.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age in their country). The Service is not intended for anyone younger. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. International Transfers

The Service may be hosted and processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version in this repository and update the "Last updated" date. For material changes, we will announce them in our support server. Continued use of the Bot after changes means you accept the updated policy.
